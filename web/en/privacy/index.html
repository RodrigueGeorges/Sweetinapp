Privacy policy

What Sweetin does with your data, and what it doesn’t.

Version française

In short

  • A photo and its secret message are erased from our servers as soon as every recipient has scratched them, and at the latest 7 days after sending.
  • Nobody at Sweetin reads your messages, unless a recipient reports a flip.
  • No ads, no selling of data, no location. Your address book is only read if you ask, and numbers only leave your phone as encrypted fingerprints that are never kept.
  • You can delete your account at any time in the app: Friends → Settings → Delete my account.

3. What we don’t do

  • We show no ads and do not sell, rent or share your data for commercial purposes.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not access your location or your microphone. Your address book is only read when you start “Find your contacts”, and nothing from it is kept.
  • Received photos are never saved to your photo library or your phone’s storage: they are displayed, then released.
  • We make no automated decision with legal effects on you. A flip rejected by automated moderation can be challenged (see the terms of use).

4. Who can see your data

  • Your friends see your username. Recipients of a flip see the photo and message once scratched.
  • A flip sent by link can be opened by anyone who has the link: once for a private link, by everyone for 24 hours for a story. You choose who you give it to.
  • The sender of a flip sees whether you scratched it, how long it took, your reaction, and whether a screenshot was attempted.
  • The Sweetin team only accesses a flip’s content if it has been reported.
  • Our technical providers, who process data only on our instructions:

5. Transfers outside the European Union

Some providers are located in the United States. These transfers are covered by the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses and, where the provider participates, by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. You can request a copy by writing to us.

6. Security

  • Traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Photos are stored in a private space and are only accessible to the recipient, through a temporary link valid for 2 minutes.
  • The secret message is stored separately and can only be read by the recipient when opening the flip.
  • Database access rules prevent any user from reading another user’s data.
  • Screenshots are blocked on Android and reported to the sender on iOS. No technique can stop someone from photographing the screen with another device, though: don’t send anything you wouldn’t want kept.

7. Minimum age

Sweetin is for people aged 16 and over. Date of birth is requested at sign-up; under 16, the account cannot be used and must be deleted. If you believe a child under 16 is using Sweetin, write to us and we will delete the account.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, port and object to the processing of your data, and to set instructions for what happens to it after your death.

  • Delete your account: directly in the app, or by following these instructions.
  • Turn off statistics: Settings → Anonymous statistics.
  • Stop being findable by your number: Settings → Findable by my number.
  • Turn off notifications: in your phone’s settings.
  • Any other request: write to team@sweetin.app from your account’s email address, stating your username. We reply within one month.

If you believe your rights are not respected, you can complain to the French data protection authority, the CNIL (cnil.fr), or to the authority of your country of residence.

9. Phone permissions

  • Camera: to take a flip’s photo. Nothing is recorded without your action.
  • Photos: only the picture you choose from your library.
  • Contacts (optional): to find your friends, only when you ask.
  • Notifications: to be told about flips you receive and about Sweet Hour.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. For any significant change, we will let you know in the app before it takes effect. The last update date is shown at the top of this page.
