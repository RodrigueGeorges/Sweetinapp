Child safety standards

Our standards against child sexual abuse and exploitation, and how to report it.

Version française

In short

  • Sweetin has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming and sextortion.
  • Sweetin is for people 16 and older. Age is checked at sign-up and, where the law requires it, with the age range provided by the App Store or Google Play.
  • Any such content is removed, the account is closed and the facts are reported to the relevant authorities.

Last updated: October 4, 2026

what is not allowed

  • Any image, video or text depicting or describing a minor in a sexual way, real or generated.
  • Any conversation aimed at approaching a minor for sexual purposes, obtaining images of them or blackmailing them.
  • Sharing or promoting links to such content.

how we prevent it

  • Minimum age of 16, checked by the server; a refusal is final.
  • Automatic moderation before delivery of every photo, every message and every profile photo. Anything suspected of involving a minor is blocked, even at low confidence.
  • No messages from strangers: you only receive secrets from friends, or through a link a friend chose to share.
  • In-app reporting: on every secret (“…” menu while reading) and every profile (Friends → “…” → Report). Reporting immediately blocks the person and keeps the content for our team.
  • Blocking any user at any time.

what we do when it is reported

  • The content is removed and the account suspended during review, within 24 hours.
  • Child sexual abuse material is reported to NCMEC (CyberTipline) and, in France, to PHAROS; we cooperate with law enforcement as required by law.
  • Evidence needed for investigations is stored securely for as long as the law requires.

report

  • In the app: “…” menu on a secret, or “…” on a friend → Report.
  • By email: safety@sweetin.app (reply within 24 hours).
  • If a child is in immediate danger, call your local emergency number (911 in the US, 112 in Europe).
